In a decaying world overrun bu the dead, where every corpse hungers for your flesh, the living aren't necessarily your only enemy. Sometimes, the greatest threat comes lies much closer to home... lurking right between your legs.

[b]What else?[/b] [spoiler]Find it yourself 😆[/spoiler]

[b]Features:[/b]
This addon adds corniness as a debuff rather than just harmless mood swing.

It slows down your movement, drains endurance, and affects your comfort, temperature, panic, and even aim. Sleep might help reduce your craving but not at extreme levels.

Gooning wipes the craving, calms your head, clears some stress, boredom, and unhappiness, and gives you a temporary endurance boost. Ignore it and the pressure builds every day, ramping up for the first 14 days of deprivation.

To relieve yourself:
[olist]
[*]Feed the craving by [i]sniffing[/i] certain underwear or reading HottieZ.
[*]Once you see the corny moodle, find a lubricant and get to work.
[*]Open your inventory, pick the lubricant, and choose [b]Goon[/b] — Keep your lower body unobstructed.
[/olist]
