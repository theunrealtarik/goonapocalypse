In a decaying world overrun by the dead, every corpse wants your flesh. But the real danger might be much closer to home

[b]Features:[/b]
[list]
[*]Adds a deprivation system built around a growing craving and a corny moodle
[*]Horniness builds over time and from certain stimuli, including sniffing underwear and reading HottieZ
[*]At higher levels, the craving slows movement, drains endurance, hurts comfort and temperature, raises panic, and reduces aim accuracy
[*]Relief clears the craving, reduces stress, boredom, and unhappiness, and gives a temporary endurance boost
[*]Gooning only works when the player has a valid lubricant and an unobstructed lower body
[*]Sleep can reduce the craving, but it does not fully solve it at extreme levels
[*]The pressure ramps up daily for the first 14 days of deprivation, then peaks harder the longer it is ignored
[/list]

To relieve yourself:
[olist]
[*]Get your self in the mood by either sniffing certain underwear or reading HottieZ
[*]Grind your way up waiting for the corny moodle to appear
[*]Use a proper lubricant from your inventory and choose [b]Goon[/b]
[*]Keep your lower body clear and your hands available
[/olist]
